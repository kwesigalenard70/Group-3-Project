Our Mission

Healing. Discovery.
Education.

We are committed to improving the health of the community and the world by setting the standard of excellence in medical education, research, and clinical care.

Our faculty, students, and staff work together to translate laboratory discoveries into treatments, and treatments into healthier lives — for patients here at home and across the globe.

  • 🔬
    Scientific Excellence Rigorous inquiry and evidence-based practice drive every decision we make at the bedside and bench.
  • 🤝
    Patient-Centred Care Every patient is a person, not a chart. We listen, partner, and advocate across the full care journey.
  • 🌍
    Inclusive Community Diversity of thought, background, and experience makes our research more powerful and our care more equitable.
  • 💡
    Relentless Innovation We chase the questions no one else has answered yet — because lives depend on what we discover tomorrow.
Our History

A Legacy Built Over Generations

From a single hospital in Baltimore to a global health system — each decade a new chapter in medicine.

1889

Doors Open

The Johns Hopkins Hospital admits its first patients in Baltimore, Maryland.

1893

Medical School

The School of Medicine opens, pioneering the residency training model still used worldwide.

1970s

Research Era

Landmark discoveries in oncology and cardiology reshape global treatment standards.

Today

Global Reach

Six hospitals, hundreds of clinics, and partnerships in more than 100 countries.
