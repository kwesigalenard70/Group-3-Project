Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions patients ask first.

How quickly can I book a first appointment?

Most specialists offer same-week appointments when slots are available. Use the filter to find doctors with open schedules.

Can I consult a doctor remotely?

Yes. Doctors with telehealth availability are clearly labelled on their cards.

What insurance partners do you accept?

We accept cash, mobile money, and major insurers including Jubilee Insurance, UAP Old Mutual, Liberty Life Uganda and AAR Health.

Is there support if I need urgent help?

Yes. Call our care team at +256 700 000 000 or email care@lifespringhospital.org for same-day assistance.

Why Choose Life Spring Hospital?

  • Highly qualified specialists with international training
  • Multiple branches across Uganda for convenient access
  • Flexible appointment and payment options for every patient
